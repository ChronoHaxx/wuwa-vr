Capture guide

Record first-person clips

Show what WuWa VR feels like: your character's hands, outfit and idle animation at full scale. This guide uses the tools that ship with WuWa VR; no extra capture software is needed for the basic route.

01

Before you start

  • Install and launch through the WuWa VR app as usual. See the three setup steps.
  • The built-in recorder captures the game's two eye views from SteamVR or the bundled OpenXR Simulator. It does not record the desktop or audio. VDXR is not supported.
  • Recording costs performance. Close other heavy apps, and expect a lower frame rate than normal play.
  • Recordings stop after five minutes, when the game closes or when the scene changes.
02

Get into first person

Press L3 + View to enter first person. In UEVR settings (L3 + R3), open VR → WuWa Controls → First person and choose to hide supported head bones so the body stays visible.

If the view clips into the body, raise or lower it with L3 + Y / L3 + X. For a livelier idle shot, L3 + D-pad Down toggles full animation follow. It moves the view with the character's head, so it is more intense; keep takes short or sit down.

03

Clean up the frame

  • Close UEVR settings with L3 + R3.
  • Hide the shortcut sheet with L3 + Menu.
  • Hide the game UI with L3 + B. Press it again to bring the UI back.
  • Optional: WuWa Controls → Streamer privacy: cover player IDs masks your UID. Still review clips for names before sharing.

Then stand still and let the idle animation play. Move your head slowly: look at your hands, then down at the outfit, then out at the scenery.

04

Make it sharper (optional)

In UEVR settings, open OpenXR Options → Resolution Scale and raise it above 1.0 (try 1.3–1.5). The game renders more pixels per eye and the recording looks cleaner, at a GPU cost. Lower it again if the frame rate drops. If nothing changes, restart the game.

Raising in-game graphics quality helps too. Lighting and far detail already match between the eyes.

05

Record

  1. With the game running, select Developer tools in the WuWa VR app's footer. The advanced launcher opens in your browser.
  2. Under Record gameplay, set Purpose to Content creation: clean video only.
  3. Pick Resolution per eye: 1280 px. Use 30 fps to start; try 45 or 60 only if your PC has headroom. The recorder cannot add frames the game never rendered.
  4. Select Start recording, return to the game and play. Select Stop recording when done, then Open recordings.

Simulator capture: keep the simulator preview open with both eyes side by side and full render off. Its video resolution is limited by the preview window size.

06

Export one eye

The recorder saves clean-sbs.mp4, a side-by-side stereo video. For a normal flat video, keep one eye. replay.html in the same folder can save single-eye stills. To crop the video with FFmpeg, run this in PowerShell from the recording folder:

ffmpeg -i clean-sbs.mp4 -vf "crop=iw/2:ih:0:0" -c:v libx264 -crf 18 -preset slow left-eye.mp4

Need more than 1280 px? Open SteamVR's Display VR View, show one eye, maximize the window and record it with OBS. That captures the mirror at your monitor's resolution, including any resolution scale you set.

Share what you make. A clip of first-person idle animation in your favourite outfit is a great way to show people the mod. Please credit WuWa VR and keep the account-risk notice in mind when posting gameplay.
