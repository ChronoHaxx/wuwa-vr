Record gameplay

Record side-by-side video through SteamVR or the updated OpenXR Simulator. The source is detected for the running game. No desktop or audio capture; VDXR capture is not supported yet.

Simulator: keep the preview open with Both eyes, side-by-side and Full render off. Video resolution is limited by the preview size. The replay can export single-eye or stereo stills.

Choose content creation for older builds without camera/controller recording.

30 fps is a useful starting point for 30-45 fps gameplay. Higher targets need more headroom. The recorder reports its measured capture rate; choosing 60 cannot create game frames that were never rendered. Video has no diagnostic labels burned in, in either mode.

Stops after five minutes, game exit or a scene change. Keep footage private until you have reviewed it for player names/UIDs. Recording does not play the game or change camera settings.

Recovery and removal


Undo, reset or remove

Reset this build replaces the chosen build's settings with the ones it was supplied with. Your current settings are backed up first.


Restore my settings from before WuWa VR puts your UEVR profile and injector choice back to how they were before this launcher first changed them. The mod's settings are kept in a backup, so you can apply the build again later.


To remove WuWa VR completely: select Use headset if the simulator is active, restore your settings, choose Stop launcher, then delete the WuWa VR folder. Backups and logs remain in the data folder until you delete it too.

Compare stereo rendering (experimental)

For the prepared stereo-labels candidate running through SteamVR. Open a scene showing the doubled reflections or one-eye material, close the UEVR overlay, then start below and return to WuWa. The batch waits for game focus; keep the view still until it finishes.


Five reflection/material settings are tested separately, with before/changed/restored images. Each original value is restored before the next test. This helps diagnose a defect; it does not apply a permanent fix or change the VR runtime.


The planar setting can stop updates while leaving a cached reflection visible. Unchanged images do not rule that rendering path out.




Captures include the HUD and may include your UID. Exact frame pairing between eyes is unavailable. Results remain local; review them before sharing.
