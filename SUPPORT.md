# Optional support

**There is no obligation to donate. Please do not donate if you expect me to
support or maintain this mod forever.** This is a spare-time hobby: I cannot
promise updates, personal help, a response time or repairs after game updates,
and I may pause or stop. Only contribute if you are happy to support the work as
it is, with nothing owed in return.

I build VR experiments, game mods and small tools because I enjoy using them.
Support goes toward a GPU upgrade (more headroom for modded VR and for running
open-weight AI models locally, so development depends less on hosted tools) and
the time and equipment these projects take. It does not buy priority fixes,
deadlines or guaranteed compatibility.

Downloads stay free, and support never limits access or participation. Our
original work has a [scoped MIT license](LICENSE.md); upstream code keeps its
own terms. Please keep community access free and credit the original creators.

## Ways to help

- Share a clip of the current build, crediting the upstream creators and
  removing personal account details.
- Report a reproducible bug with the build name and headset software.
- Contribute translations, documentation, accessibility feedback or fixes.
- Support the original authors through links on their own pages.

Optional tips: [Ko-fi / ChronoHax](https://ko-fi.com/chronohax). Payments are
handled by Ko-fi; this website collects no payment details.
