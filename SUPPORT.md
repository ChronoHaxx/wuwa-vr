# Optional support

**There is no obligation to donate. Please do not donate if you expect me to
support or maintain this mod forever.** This is a spare-time hobby; I cannot
promise continued updates, personal support, a response time, or repairs after
future game updates. I may need to pause or stop work. Only contribute if you
are comfortable supporting the work as it is, without anything owed in return.

I make VR experiments, game mods and small tools because I enjoy building
things I want to use. If something here makes your games more enjoyable,
optional support would help me spend more time testing and improving it.

My next hardware goal is a GPU upgrade: more room to run open-weight models
locally, less dependence on hosted tools, and more headroom for modded VR games.
I currently use hosted coding tools too; the goal is to make local development
more practical, not to claim that this work already runs entirely on local AI.

Support would go toward that hardware goal and the time/equipment involved in
these hobby projects. It would not buy priority fixes, a release deadline or
guaranteed compatibility. Any future public downloads are intended to remain
separate from optional support, subject to the original authors' permissions.

The project is free and fan-made. Help, feedback and community participation
are welcome regardless of whether you contribute money. Original independent
work has a [scoped MIT license](LICENSE.md); upstream code retains its own
terms. Please keep community access free and credit the original creators.

## Ways to help

- Share a clip that accurately shows the current build, with the upstream
  creators credited and personal account details removed.
- Report a reproducible bug with its checkpoint and headset/runtime.
- Contribute documentation, accessibility feedback or fixes where the project
  and upstream licensing allow them.
- Support the original authors through links they publish on their own pages.

Optional tips: [Ko-fi / ChronoHax](https://ko-fi.com/chronohax).
Payments are handled by Ko-fi and its payment providers. The project website
does not collect payment details. Support never limits access or participation.
