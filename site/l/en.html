Get started

  1. Extract the whole ZIP to a normal folder, then open WuWa VR Launcher.exe. Keep the app and python folders beside it. You do not need to install Python.
  2. Connect your headset and start its software. To use the simulator, close WuWa and the injector, then select Use this package's simulator. Switching the system OpenXR runtime asks for Windows permission and takes effect on the next game launch.
  3. Read and accept the risk notice, choose a build, then Apply & launch. Accept Windows permission for injection and press Play in the game launcher. The standalone official launcher is the tested route; Steam/Epic injection remains unverified.
  4. L3 + R3 opens UEVR. L3 + B shows/hides HUD and menus; L3 + A recenters; L3 + Menu shows the shortcut sheet; L3 + LB toggles HUD / mouse mode. Close UEVR settings before using gameplay shortcuts.

Everyday Xbox controls

L3 / R3 mean pressing the sticks. View is the two-squares button; Menu is the three-lines button. LB + Y stays the game's normal action.

Comfort and recovery

In HUD placement and visibility, choose a following HUD, adjust size and distance together, or fit it to the optional portal. L3 + B restores a hidden menu. L3 + A recenters your view. The sheet starts enabled in the new supplied profile; L3 + Menu hides it. Existing saved choices are respected.

What still needs work

Use the settings supplied with your build; Native Stereo Fix defaults differ between releases. BUILD GUIDE.html in newer packages lists those values. Reflections, visibility and lighting differences between eyes, and full-animation aiming remain under test. Candidate fixes are not confirmed for every scene. No global time pause is implemented.
