WuWa VR beta - Steam and Kuro - 6 October 2026
Launcher 1.0.2 | VR package beta-2026-10-06-steam | game 3.7

Download WuWa-VR-Setup.exe, run it, then open WuWa VR.
1. Choose your detected Kuro or Steam installation in step 01.
2. Confirm your game version and install the VR package in step 02.
3. Start your headset software, check the runtime, then Launch in VR.
Kuro opens its launcher; press Play there. Steam starts the selected game through
Steam. Windows permission, Steam sign-in or game updates may still be needed.

Already installed using Setup? The app checks for updates at startup. In step 02,
Versions & updates > Check updates can also refresh both update lists. Choose
Update launcher; when ready and the game/injector/recording are stopped, choose
Restart to update. Then select beta-2026-10-06-steam in step 02 and install it.
Updating the app keeps your existing VR package selection and settings.
A portable EXE does not self-update; use Setup for installed-app updates.

What changed
- Steam installation detection and explicit Kuro/Steam choice. The Steam route
  targets that installation's actual game process and refuses ambiguous targets.
- Steam choices survive upgrading an older VR package.
- Graphics now follow the game's settings instead of silently writing old low
  or medium startup overrides. Recognized inherited overrides are backed up and
  removed; custom overrides remain explicit. No universal quality preset is imposed.
- The player menu groups ordinary controls and keeps experimental diagnostics
  separate. Optional walking input can merge VR buttons with a selected treadmill
  or Xbox slot; Xbox remains the main gameplay option.
- Prior cinematic framing, mono/stereo screen, portal, diorama and accepted
  foliage/lighting corrections are retained. Renderer unchanged from graphics-r2.

Evidence and remaining limits
The owner reported the Steam game running; its backend startup and stereo activity
were observed. Background discovery, exact-path injection policy, upgrade handoff,
graphics migration, packaging and offscreen UI checks are covered. Other PCs,
physical headset comfort, all controls and normal Steam exit need further testing.
The launch status can remain waiting for UEVR even after backend startup.
Long scene/dialogue stalls, menu backgrounds moving separately from the HUD,
unreliable HUD refresh and some stereo effects remain open. Automatic cinema is
experimental, off by default, and actual prerecorded movie detection is unverified.
No claim is made that this fixes Indath's initialization crash.

Manual fallback: hold LT + RT, then click R3 for mono theatre. Hold L3 for 0.8s
with both triggers held for the stereo screen. Release controls before repeating.

Previous releases remain available for rollback. Your data stays in the existing
WuWa VR Manager and WuWa VR Launcher folders under LocalAppData.
For reports use Troubleshooting > Copy diagnostics and include the selected package,
game storefront, headset/runtime, what happened, and any displayed error.

Advanced portable fallback: fully extract WuWa-VR-Launcher.zip and open
WuWa VR Launcher.exe. Keep app and python together. This uses the developer web UI.
